End-to-End Workflow

The FoodBridge Rescue Lifecycle

Designed specifically to prevent commercial waste while strictly adhering to municipal health codes, thermal control standards, and verified charity distribution.

Understanding the Platform Scope

✅ What FoodBridge IS
  • Surplus Food Coordination: A direct channel for commercial food providers with unexpected leftovers.
  • Free Community Donation: Zero fees, zero commission, and zero cost to donors or receiving charities.
  • NGO Verification Gateway: Only registered community kitchens and shelters can request collections.
  • HACCP Transparency: Mandatory declarations for allergens, storage temperatures, and preparation windows.
❌ What FoodBridge IS NOT
  • NOT Food Delivery or Ordering: We do not offer courier gig delivery, customer ordering, or takeaway dining.
  • NOT for Expired or Spoiled Food: Food that has degraded, spoiled, or breached safe temperature limits must NOT be donated.
  • NOT an Anonymous Free-for-all: Private individuals cannot randomly claim food; accountability is strictly maintained.
  • NOT a Replacement for Good Practices: Software coordinates data, but human operators must inspect food on-site.
01
For Donors

Listing Surplus Food with Complete Data

When banquet halls, university dining halls, or restaurants finish a service with untouched surplus, they log into FoodBridge. The donation form requires:

• Preparation Timestamp: Exact time cooking or baking was completed.
• Thermal Storage: Hot warmer (>60°C), Chilled (4°C), Frozen, or Dry storage.
• Allergen Matrix: Declaration of gluten, nuts, dairy, eggs, etc.
• Pickup Deadline: Strict window before quality decreases.
02
For Verified NGOs

Surplus Discovery & Request Submission

Accredited community organisations monitor the real-time directory. When a relevant batch is discovered, the NGO submits a request specifying:

• Vehicle & Equipment: Insulated thermal bags, refrigerated van, or food crates.
• Estimated Arrival Time: Realistic pickup window matching the donor's schedule.
• Beneficiary Planning: Target number of meals served (e.g. 50 transition housing residents).
03
Coordination

Donor Acceptance & Private Logistics Release

The food donor reviews incoming requests on their dashboard. Once the donor clicks Accept:

• The exact service dock address, security gate instructions, and kitchen manager contact are securely revealed to the assigned NGO.
• The listing status updates from Available to Accepted, preventing multiple pickups.

04
Verification at Handover

Physical Handover & Impact Logging

At the pickup location, the collecting volunteer and the chef or kitchen lead verify the containers. Once handed over:

• The donor or NGO marks the item as Collected.
• Rescued kilograms are credited to the donor's sustainability records, automatically calculating diverted landfill waste and saved greenhouse emissions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are donors protected against food safety liability?

Yes. In most modern legal jurisdictions, Good Samaritan food donation statutes protect donors who act in good faith from liability unless there is gross negligence or intentional misconduct. FoodBridge requires clear disclosures to further uphold this standard.

Who can register as an NGO on FoodBridge?

Only legally registered non-profit organisations, community soup kitchens, registered shelters, and verified student welfare pantries. Organizations must provide their official registration number for platform admin review before requesting collections.

What types of food can be donated?

Unserved cooked buffet batches, daily bakery surplus, unblemished wholesale produce crates, short-dated sealed dairy and packaged staples, and unopened canned foods. Leftovers from customer plates or food that was left uncovered at room temperature are strictly prohibited.
