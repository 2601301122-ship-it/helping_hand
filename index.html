The Challenge & The Solution

Bridging the Gap Between Surplus and Scarcity

Every day, tons of perfectly edible food are discarded while community organizations struggle to meet growing nutrition demands.

🗑️

The Surplus Food Problem

  • Unpredictable Event Surplus: Banquets, hotels, and college hostels routinely prepare for maximum capacity, resulting in unserved batches.
  • Short Shelf-Life Vulnerability: Bakeries and supermarkets discard unsold culinary goods that have hours of prime freshness remaining.
  • Lack of Real-Time Coordination: Kitchens lack a verified channel to quickly notify reliable grassroots charities before food deteriorates.
  • FSSAI Compliance Concern: Many donors avoid donation fearing regulatory confusion around the Surplus Food Donation Regulations 2019.
🌱

The FoodBridge Answer

  • Instant Surplus Publication: Donors post quantity, preparation time, storage requirements, and allergen disclosures in seconds.
  • Vetted NGO Coordination: Only accredited, verified non-profits with safe transport can claim and collect donations.
  • QR Collection Verification: Each accepted handover generates a unique token for transparent pickup confirmation.
  • Zero Ordering or Delivery Fee: We are NOT a commercial food ordering app — our mission is strictly community waste reduction.
Simple & Transparent

How FoodBridge Operates

A reliable, four-step rescue workflow designed around local food safety regulations including FSSAI guidelines.

1

Donor Publishes Surplus

Restaurant, banquet or supermarket enters food details, prep time, temperature conditions, allergens, and collection deadline.

2

Verified NGO Requests

Accredited community shelters review local listings, confirm thermal transport capacity, and submit a collection request.

3

Donor Accepts & Unlocks

Donor approves the request. Private loading dock address is released securely only to the assigned organisation.

4

QR Verification & Impact Log

A unique collection token is generated. Food is safely collected, and weight is credited to impact metrics automatically.

Food Safety & Responsible Donation Notice:

FoodBridge coordinates surplus food discovery but does not certify microbiological safety or temperature history. Donors must comply with FSSAI Surplus Food Donation Regulations 2019. Collecting NGOs must perform sensory and temperature checks prior to serving. FoodBridge is NOT responsible for food quality after handover.
