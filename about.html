Our Genesis & Mission

Bridging the Divide Between Commercial Surplus and Community Hunger

FoodBridge was created to solve a tragic structural inefficiency: massive amounts of safe, freshly prepared food are discarded every evening while local community shelters face food supply shortages.

🌱

Food Waste Reduction

Food waste accounts for 8-10% of global greenhouse emissions. Diverting untouched commercial food directly from landfills protects our planet and saves municipal disposal resources.

🤝

Verified Grassroots NGOs

We do not distribute food to unverified entities. Every non-profit partner is audited for tax status, food handling hygiene, and verifiable community soup kitchen operations.

🛡️

Transparent Food Safety

Donations require mandatory preparation timestamps, temperature protocols (hot warmer vs refrigerated), and allergen disclosures. Private addresses are protected until coordination is confirmed.

⚡

Rapid Time-to-Rescue

Surplus food has a strict culinary shelf-life. Our instant notification architecture connects kitchens with transport-equipped community teams in minutes, not days.

Global Impact

Directly Advancing UN Sustainable Development Goals

FoodBridge aligns with global benchmarks established by the United Nations:

SDG 2
Zero Hunger

Ensuring nutritious food reaches vulnerable community members through soup kitchens, youth shelters, and senior outreach programs.

SDG 12
Responsible Consumption & Production

Halving per capita food waste at retail and food service levels by transitioning surplus into immediate community nourishment.

Good Samaritan Legal Protections & Food Safety Policy

In many jurisdictions, food businesses hesitate to donate due to liability concerns. FoodBridge is built upon established Good Samaritan Food Donation legislation (such as the US Bill Emerson Good Samaritan Food Donation Act and international equivalents) which shields honest donors from civil and criminal liability when donating apparently wholesome food in good faith.

Clear Distinction of Responsibility:

FoodBridge operates as a coordination software platform. FoodBridge does not prepare, transport, or certify food. Donors certify adherence to baseline hygienic standards. Receiving organisations inspect food upon handover to protect community beneficiaries.

Open Source Hackathon Project

Designed for Scalability

This platform was created as a complete college hackathon showcase. The frontend architecture is modular and ready for immediate connection to a Python Flask / SQLite backend via standard RESTful endpoints.
