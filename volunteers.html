🛡️ Food Transport Safety Requirements

FoodBridge does not certify food safety, but requires all transport volunteers to follow these minimum standards. Refer to FSSAI Surplus Food Donation Regulations 2019 for full guidelines.

❄️
Temperature Control
Cold food must be kept below 5°C. Hot food above 60°C. Use insulated carriers or cooler boxes for all perishable items.
📦
Clean Containers
Only use food-grade, clean containers. Never mix food categories. Seal all containers to prevent contamination.
⏱️
Time Limits
Cooked food must be collected and delivered within the pickup deadline window. Never transport expired or visibly spoiled food.
🧤
Personal Hygiene
Wear gloves when handling open food. Wash hands before handling containers. Do not transport food if ill.
🏷️
Labelling
Confirm that all food is labelled with the FoodBridge donation ID, preparation time, and allergen information before transporting.
📵
Private Information
Never photograph or share private donor pickup addresses. Exact location is only shared after NGO acceptance through the platform.
